A Material Initiative network project in South Tyrol.

Ehi, but what is a material initiative?

A material Initiative helps the “digestion“ of material offcuts in a territory.

Is a physical place where materials of every kind (besides toxic and dangerous one), that are still in good condition, are stored and organized in order to be available again for reuse purposes.

Therefore they are useful to reduce waste production, promote sustainability and enhance circular economy.

South Tyrol does not sit around.

We can count already 3 running initiative - Bolzano, Bressanone and Lana - and 1 starting soon in Silandro.

Impact on the economy - a practical example:

A small workshop for a class of 20 children, with a consume of 0,5 m² per children of plywood, would cost 529€ for the material only. This cost would be entirely avoidable by recovering offcuts daily produced by carpentry.

Our Proposal

Building a centralized space (ideally in Bolzano) for reuse in South Tyrol, with a dedicated and fixed staff, would help vastly material recovery and redistribution on the territory:

Moving
in the same direction.

A headquarter would allow sharing practices and methodologies, ensuring the quality of material storing and cataloguation.

This would allow the creation of a rich and diverse program for sustainability material-driven education - giving also opportunity for volunteering or Young Action project.

A space for synergies.

We are looking for a deposit area of 200m² with scaffolding to store and sell different kind of materials.

Fundamental would be the realization of creative spaces for workshops, group activities and generally upcycling activities leaded by creative and Designers thanks to a collaboration with the University.

A coherent system.

Payments, tools, staff and spaces are going to follow the same principles, making material more accessible and easy to distribute.
