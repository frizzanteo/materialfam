A Material Initiative network project in South Tyrol.

Ehi, but what is a material initiative?

A material Initiative supports a healthy flow of material offcuts in a territory.

Is a physical place where materials of every kind, that are still in good condition, are stored and organized in order to be available again for reuse purposes.

Therefore they are useful to reduce waste production, promote sustainability and enhance circular economy. In short, closing the loop!

South Tyrol does not sit around.

We can count already three running initiatives in Bolzano, Bressanone and Lana - and one coming up in Silandro.

Impact on the economy - a practical example:

A small workshop for a class of 20 children, with a consume of 0,5 m² per children of plywood, would cost more than 500€ for the material only. This cost would be entirely avoidable by recovering offcuts daily produced by carpentry.

Our Proposal

Building a centralized space (ideally in Bolzano) for reuse in South Tyrol, with a dedicated and fixed staff, would help vastly material recovery and redistribution on the territory:

Moving
in the same direction.

A headquarter would allow sharing practices and methodologies, ensuring the quality of material storing and cataloguation.

This would allow the creation of a rich and diverse program for sustainability material-driven education - giving also opportunity for volunteering or Young Action project.

A space for synergies.

We are looking for a deposit area of approximate 200m² with scaffolding to store and sell different kind of materials.

Fundamental woul be the realization of creative spaces for workshops, group activities and general upcycling activities. For instance, these could be lead by creative professionals and young designers by collaborating with the Free University of Bozen-Bolzano.

A coherent system.

Payments, tools, staff and spaces are going to follow the same principles, making material more accessible and easy to distribute.
